# EMBEDDER_DECISION.md

Embedder selection benchmark for the Phase-1 RAG rebuild.
Generated by `scripts/benchmark_embedders.py`.

## Method

- **Recall subset**: seeded sample from `data/learning/training_scenarios_drive_archive_v2.jsonl`. Each row's ground-truth chunk is indexed under its doc_id/chunk_index, then the question is queried at the same embedder dimension.
- **Recall metric**: doc/chunk recall@5.
- **Fresh-upload wins**: the 12 single-chunk project-specific notes from `scripts/rag_fresh_upload_eval.py`. PASS = fresh doc is top-1 (top-3 tracked).
- **Fresh metric**: top-1 / top-3 fresh wins out of 12.
- Each candidate runs in its own throwaway namespace on the fixed namespace-aware vector store; the legacy `chunks` table is untouched.

## Results

| Model | Dim | Load (s) | Index (s) | doc@5 | chunk@5 | fresh top-1 | fresh top-3 |
|-------|-----|----------|-----------|--------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| minishlab/potion-base-8M | 256 | 39.5 | 0.5 | 0.95 | 0.55 | 12/12 | 12/12 |
| sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 | 384 | 8.2 | 9.4 | 0.90 | 0.70 | 12/12 | 12/12 |
| BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 | 384 | 8.6 | 17.6 | 0.95 | 0.65 | 12/12 | 12/12 |

## Recommendation (draft for Chadi)

**Winner: `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`**

- Best doc recall@5 (0.95, tied with potion), strong chunk recall@5 (0.65),
  and perfect fresh-upload wins (12/12 top-1).
- 384-dim vectors fit comfortably inside Render's memory envelope
  (~130 MB model weights, ~50 % larger than MiniLM, much smaller than
  any base-sized model).
- Trade-off: indexing is slower than potion (17.6 s vs 0.5 s for this
  small subset). For the full ~142k-chunk re-index this will translate to
  hours, not minutes — acceptable for a one-time Phase-1 rebuild.

**Runner-up: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`**

- Highest chunk recall@5 (0.70), same perfect fresh-upload score, faster
  load than bge-small, but doc recall@5 is lower (0.90).
- Choose this if Render memory or index throughput becomes the binding
  constraint during the full re-index.

**Not selected: `minishlab/potion-base-8M`**

- Extremely fast indexing and smallest footprint, but chunk recall@5
  (0.55) is materially lower than the 384-dim candidates. Doc recall is
  good (0.95), but the pilot needs both doc- and chunk-level precision.

**Render resource fit**

| Model | Params | Approx. weights | Dim | Estimated peak RAM* |
|-------|--------|-----------------|-----|---------------------|
| potion-base-8M | 8 M | ~30 MB | 256 | ~300 MB |
| all-MiniLM-L6-v2 | 22 M | ~80 MB | 384 | ~400 MB |
| bge-small-en-v1.5 | 33 M | ~130 MB | 384 | ~500 MB |

\* Peak RAM measured informally on this workstation (Python 3.11, CPU-only
PyTorch). All three are well within Render's starter/plus tiers; the
service should be sized to at least 1 GB RAM to leave headroom for the
embedding batch + the application.
