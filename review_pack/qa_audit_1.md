## QA Audit Programme for Piling Subcontractor

### 1. Audit Scope
The scope of the quality audit programme for the piling subcontractor on the Diriyah Gate II Infrastructure Package 1 project includes:
- Review of piling work in accordance with the Subcontract and applicable standards.
- Verification of piling work fitness for purpose.

### 2. Audit Frequency
The audit frequency will be determined based on the project's specific requirements.

### 3. Piling-Specific Quality Hold Points and Witness Points
The audit programme will include piling-specific quality hold points and witness points as follows:
- Concrete pour: 
  - Hold point: Before concrete pour.
  - Witness point: During concrete pour.
- Rebar installation: 
  - Hold point: Before rebar cage installation.
  - Witness point: During rebar cage installation.

### 4. ITP Requirements
The audit programme will include ITP (Inspection and Test Plan) requirements as follows:
- Concrete testing: 
  - Compressive strength tests.
  - Slump tests.
- Rebar testing: 
  - Tensile strength tests.
  - Bend tests.

### 5. Material Testing Requirements
The audit programme will include material testing requirements as follows:
- Concrete: 
  - Compressive strength testing in accordance with [DD-2023-118_Diriyah Gate II Infrastructure Package 1_Vol 1.0_Cond of Contract (complete).pdf, Clause 9.4].
- Rebar: 
  - Tensile strength testing in accordance with [DD-2023-118_Diriyah Gate II Infrastructure Package 1_Vol 1.0_Cond of Contract (complete).pdf, Clause 9.4].

### 6. Installation Tolerances
The audit programme will include installation tolerances as follows:
- Verticality tolerance: ± 1% of pile depth.
- Location tolerance: ± 50 mm.

### 7. Record-Keeping Requirements
The audit programme will include record-keeping requirements as follows:
- Daily records of piling activities.
- Records of nonconformance and corrective actions.

### 8. Nonconformance Procedures
The audit programme will include nonconformance procedures as follows:
- Identification of nonconforming work.
- Isolation of nonconforming work.
- Corrective actions.

### 9. Structured Audit Checklist
The audit programme will include a structured audit checklist as follows:
- Piling activity: 
  - Checklist item 1: Concrete pour.
  - Checklist item 2: Rebar installation.
