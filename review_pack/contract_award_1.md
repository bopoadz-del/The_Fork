## Answer (verbatim)

**Letter of Award (LOA)**

The LOA is the **Employer’s written notice of acceptance** to the Consultant. Once the Consultancy Agreement is executed and delivered, that written notice together with the **Consultant’s acceptance** of it “shall constitute a legally binding contract between the Employer and the Consultant valid from the date of the Employer’s written Letter of Award (LOA).”

The context also states that the **issuance of a LOA does not mean the Employer accepts any and all content of the Consultant’s proposal**; acceptance will be subject to the terms and conditions of the agreement.  
*(Source: RFP for Structural Design and Engineering Services, chunk 58)*

**Bonds required before project initiation**

The following bonds are listed as **Project Initiation Requirements**:

1. **Performance Bond** – must match the issued form of bond under **(6.08)** and must be valid **until 30 days after the date of the Performance Certificate**.  
2. **Advance Payment Bond** – must match the issued form of bond under **Vol - 6.08 sch. 8**; the bond shall be in an amount equivalent to the **total Advance Payment** (**Cls 4.3.2**). Where the bond is subject to a fixed expiry date, the required validity period under the Contract may extend beyond that date.  
*(Source: Pre-Kick off meeting – Construction, chunks 12 and 14)*

**Other initiation conditions**

The same initiation requirements note that submission and acceptance by **DGCL** of the following insurance documentation is a condition: **Automobile Liability, Aviation Liability Insurance, Marine Cargo Insurance, and Professional Indemnity Insurance**.  
*(Source: Pre-Kick off meeting – Construction, chunk 12)*
