### Introduction

[Employer's Company Name] is seeking proposals from qualified subcontractors to provide landscaping services for the [Project Name] at [Project Location]. ### Scope of Work

The successful subcontractor will be responsible for:

1. Reviewing and adhering to the project's environmental and sustainability requirements.
2. Conducting a site survey to verify existing conditions and identifying any potential challenges or opportunities.
3. Providing a detailed plan for landscaping, including plant selection, soil preparation, irrigation systems, and hardscaping features.
4. Obtaining all necessary permits and approvals.
5. Executing the landscaping work in accordance with the approved plan, project specifications, and relevant regulatory requirements.
6. Providing ongoing maintenance services for a period specified in the contract.

### Proposal Requirements

Proposers must submit the following:

1. **Company Profile:** A brief overview of the company, including experience in similar landscaping projects, qualifications, and relevant certifications.
2. **Project Team:** Names and resumes of key personnel who will be involved in the project, including their roles and responsibilities.
3. **Scope of Work and Methodology:** A detailed description of how the subcontractor plans to execute the landscaping work, including timelines, milestones, and quality control measures.
4. **Pricing:** A comprehensive price list for all services, including materials, labor, equipment, and overheads. Prices should be broken down into unit costs where applicable.
5. **Schedule:** A project schedule outlining the start and completion dates, key milestones, and critical path activities.
6. **References:** Contact information for at least three (3) similar projects completed in the last five (5) years.

### Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

- Experience and qualifications of the subcontractor
- Completeness and accuracy of the proposal
- Pricing competitiveness
- Project schedule and milestones
- References and past performance
