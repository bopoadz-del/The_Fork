**REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) – LANDSCAPING SUBCONTRACT PACKAGE**

---

### 1. INVITATION TO TENDER

The Contractor invites suitably qualified landscaping subcontractors to submit a technical and commercial proposal for the **Landscaping Works** associated with the infrastructure package currently under construction.

Works under this subcontract shall be coordinated with the ongoing infrastructure trades, including roadworks, street lighting and electrical utility installations, ducting and backfilling operations, and hardscape elements such as kerbstones and concrete tiles.

---

### 2. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The project comprises civil infrastructure works including (but not limited to) road construction, excavation and backfilling, sand bedding, aggregate sub-base and base course laying, asphalt works, street lighting (poles, feeder pillars, earthing systems), electrical and communications ducting, manholes, and PVC conduit networks.

The landscaping subcontract shall integrate with the above completed (or partially completed) infrastructure elements to deliver a coordinated external-works finish.

---

### 3. SCOPE OF WORK

The Subcontractor shall carry out, as a minimum, the following:

**3.1 Soft Landscaping**
*   Supply, install, and establish topsoil, prepared and ameliorated in accordance with the specifications.
*   Supply and plant trees, shrubs, groundcover, and seasonal bedding plants as shown on the landscaping drawings and planting schedules.
*   Supply, lay, and establish seeded or turfed grass areas, including soil preparation, fertilising, and initial cutting.
*   Installation of an automatic irrigation system (if required by the specification), including trenching, supply and laying of lateral and main lines, control valves, controllers, and drip or spray emitters. Coordination with electrical trenching and ducting operations is critical.
*   Mulching of planted beds.
*   Establishment-period maintenance (watering, weeding, pest/disease control, replacement of failed plants) for the defect-liability period.

**3.2 Hard Landscaping**
*   Supply and install paving, concrete tiles, and natural stone finishes in pedestrian areas, plazas, and interface zones with roads.
*   Installation of street furniture, bollards, and protective tree grilles where shown.
*   Edging and border works abutting asphalt, concrete, or kerb lines.
*   Construction of landscape drainage falls and connection to surface water drainage systems (including coordination with existing stormwater infrastructure).

**3.3 General & Coordination**
*   Protection of existing and adjacent completed works (roads, kerbs, tiles, street-lighting poles, feeder pillars, earthing systems, ducts, and manholes) during landscaping operations.
*   Temporary hoarding, fencing, and environmental protection measures.
*   Removal of surplus spoil and construction waste off-site.
*   Handover in a clean, complete, and established condition.

---

### 4. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

*   All materials shall conform to the project specifications and applicable national standards.
*   Plant species shall be sourced from approved nurseries and shall match the specified botanical names, sizes, and quantities.
*   Soil tests (pH, salinity, organic content, drainage) shall be submitted for approval prior to topsoil placement.
*   The irrigation system shall be pressure-tested and commissioned prior to handover.
*   Hardscape tolerances (levels, falls, joint widths) shall align with the road and paving tolerances already established under the infrastructure works.
*   The Subcontractor shall maintain minimum clearances from electrical earthing systems, feeder pillars, poles, and ducting as required by the Employer's safety and utility-protection rules.

---

### 5. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Each tender submission shall include:

1.  **Company Profile** – evidence of relevant experience on infrastructure or similar landscaping projects.
2.  **Method Statement** – sequencing of works, phasing, and coordination with road/utility/street-lighting completion.
3.  **Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Plan** – risk assessments, COSHH data for chemicals/fertilisers, and proximity-work permits near live utilities.
4.  **Quality Plan** – inspection and test plans (ITPs) for soil, planting, paving, and irrigation.
5.  **Programme** – detailed bar-chart or network showing how the landscaping works integrate with the infrastructure baseline, including dependency on backfilling, road crossing ducts, and street-lighting milestones.
6.  **Manpower & Equipment Schedule**.
7.  **Commercial Proposal** – priced Bill of Quantities or Schedule of Rates, broken down by:
    *   Preliminaries and mobilisation
    *   Soft landscaping (soil, planting, turf, irrigation)
    *   Hard landscaping (paving, tiles, edging, furniture)
    *   Maintenance during defect-liability period
    *   Dayworks rates
8.  **Tender Bond / Guarantee** (if stipulated in the main contract conditions).

---

### 6. COMMERCIAL & CONTRACTUAL CONDITIONS

*   The subcontract shall be placed under back-to-back terms with the main contract where applicable.
*   Payment terms: monthly interim payments based on measured works, subject to retention and the issue of relevant payment certificates.
*   Liquidated damages provisions may apply for late completion of sections.
*   The Subcontractor shall provide performance security and insurance (public liability, employer’s liability, works / CAR) as defined in the tender enquiry documents.
*   All rates shall be firm and inclusive of labour, materials, plant, supervision, wastage, taxes, levies, and establishment costs unless otherwise stated.

---

### 7. PROGRAMME & KEY DATES

Tenderers shall note that landscaping activities are dependent on the completion of prior infrastructure trades (excavation, backfilling, ducting, cable laying, road pavement layers, and street-lighting installation). The Subcontractor shall allow for phased access and shall programme works to avoid conflict with:
*   Utility commissioning and testing.
*   Road opening or traffic-management phases.
*   Final backfilling and reinstatement of road-crossing ducts.

Key dates (indicative) will be confirmed at the tender clarification meeting:
*   Tender Issue Date: [To be confirmed]
*   Site Visit / Clarification Meeting: [To be confirmed]
*   Tender Submission Deadline: [To be confirmed]
*   Anticipated Commencement on Site: [To be confirmed]
*   Required Substantial Completion: [To be confirmed]

---

### 8. EVALUATION CRITERIA

Tenders will be evaluated on:
1.  Technical compliance and methodology (30%)
2.  Relevant experience and references (20%)
3.  Programme and integration with infrastructure works (20%)
4.  Commercial competitiveness and completeness of pricing (30%)

The Employer reserves the right to reject any tender not conforming to the minimum technical or commercial requirements.

---

### 9. TENDER ENQUIRIES & CONTACT

All enquiries shall be submitted in writing to the Employer's Representative / Procurement team. A site visit is recommended to ascertain existing conditions, access constraints, and the status of adjacent civil and electrical works (including backfilled trenches, pole locations, and manhole covers).

---

### 10. ATTACHMENTS (TO BE PROVIDED WITH THIS RFP)

1.  Form of Tender / Subcontract Agreement
2.  Drawings: Landscaping Layouts, Planting Plans, Irrigation Schematics, Hardscape Details
3.  Specifications: Landscape and Irrigation Specification
4.  Priced BOQ / Schedule of Rates template
5.  Preliminary Programme (infrastructure baseline excerpt showing relevant milestones)
6.  Health & Safety Requirements
7.  Insurances and Bond Requirements

---

**END OF RFP**
