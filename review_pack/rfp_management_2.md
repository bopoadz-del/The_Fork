**TENDER PACKAGE — ELECTRICAL WORKS**

---

## SECTION 1: SCOPE OF WORKS

### 1.1 General
The Scope of Works for the Electrical Works shall include, but not be limited to, all equipment, materials, labor, documentation, and services necessary to furnish and install a complete, operational electrical system as defined in the Contract Documents.

### 1.2 Extent of Works
The electrical works shall cover the design (where applicable), supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and handover of all electrical systems, including but not limited to:
- Medium and low voltage power distribution
- Lighting systems (internal and external)
- Earthing and lightning protection
- Fire alarm and emergency systems
- Building management system (BMS) interfaces
- Small power and general services
- Cable containment and support systems
- Low current systems (where within electrical scope)

### 1.3 Coordination
This Section shall be read in conjunction with:
- The Conditions of Contract
- The Employer's Requirements and Scope of Works
- All other related technical sections and specifications
- Drawings and schedules referenced herein or issued during the tender period

---

## SECTION 2: STANDARDS, CODES, AND REGULATIONS

### 2.1 Statutory Requirements
The work shall be in accordance with the recommendations and requirements of the following bodies, the current editions of their publications being applicable:
1. The requirements of the local authority having jurisdiction.
2. The requirements of the electrical supply authority.
3. International standards including IEC, BS, NFPA, and IEEE, where specified.

### 2.2 Conflict of Standards
In the event of conflict between standards, the following hierarchy shall apply:
1. Statutory Authority requirements and local regulations
2. Employer's Requirements and project-specific specifications
3. International standards referenced in the tender documents

---

## SECTION 3: TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### 3.1 General Requirements
- All materials and equipment shall be new, of first-class quality, and fit for purpose.
- Equipment shall be rated for the climatic and environmental conditions at the site.
- All electrical components shall carry appropriate certification (e.g., IEC, SASO, UL, or equivalent).

### 3.2 Distribution Systems
- Supply and installation of switchgear, transformers, distribution boards, and panel boards as indicated on the drawings and schedules.
- Busbars, cabling, and terminations rated for the specified fault levels and load demands.
- Circuit protective conductors and earthing systems as specified.

### 3.3 Cabling and Containment
- Power and control cables as per the Bill of Quantities and specifications.
- Cable trays, ladders, trunking, conduit, and support systems.
- Cable identification, labeling, and glanding in accordance with industry best practice.

### 3.4 Lighting and Small Power
- Luminaires, lamps, and control gear.
- Switches, sockets, and connection units.
- Emergency lighting and exit signage where required.

### 3.5 Earthing and Lightning Protection
- Main earthing terminals, earth electrodes, and bonding conductors.
- Lightning protection systems, air terminations, down conductors, and earth terminations.

---

## SECTION 4: TESTING, COMMISSIONING, AND INSPECTION

### 4.1 General
Provide all tools, equipment, labour, and materials required to perform the electrical testing and commissioning in accordance with the requirements of the Contract Documents.

### 4.2 Testing Requirements
- Include any specific testing required by the Statutory Authorities, or any other body having jurisdiction over the installation.
- Carry out testing as directed by the Employer's Representative and Assistants/Employer.
- Testing shall include, as a minimum:
  - Continuity of protective conductors
  - Insulation resistance
  - Polarity
  - Earth fault loop impedance
  - Earth electrode resistance
  - Functional testing of all systems
  - Dielectric testing of switchgear and transformers (where applicable)
  - Protection relay testing and settings verification
  - Load testing and phase balance checks

### 4.3 Commissioning
- Commissioning of Electrical Systems shall be undertaken in accordance with the minimum requirements set out in the Contract Specifications.
- Systems shall be commissioned to demonstrate full operational integrity prior to handover.

### 4.4 Test Reports
- Provide three copies of the test reports to the Employer's Representative and Assistants/Employer.
- Reports shall be in a format approved by the Employer's Representative and shall include:
  - Date and time of test
  - Equipment/system tested
  - Test method and standard applied
  - Test results and acceptable limits
  - Signature of competent person
  - Calibration certificates for test instruments

---

## SECTION 5: SUBMITTALS AND DOCUMENTATION

### 5.1 Tender Submissions
Tenderers shall submit:
- Completed Tender Form and Price Schedules
- Technical Schedule confirming compliance with Specifications
- Method Statement for installation, testing, and commissioning
- Program of Works
- List of proposed subcontractors and suppliers
- CVs and qualifications of key personnel
- Evidence of relevant experience and past performance
- Health and Safety Plan

### 5.2 Post-Award Submittals
- Shop drawings and co-ordination drawings
- Manufacturer's data sheets and O&M manuals
- As-built drawings
- Testing and commissioning records
- Training records for Employer's staff (where required)

---

## SECTION 6: QUALITY ASSURANCE

### 6.1 Quality Management
The Contractor shall implement a Quality Management System for the electrical works, including:
- Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs)
- Hold and Witness Points as defined by the Employer's Representative
- Material receiving inspections
- In-process inspections
- Final acceptance testing

### 6.2 Competency of Personnel
All electrical installation, testing, and commissioning personnel shall be appropriately qualified, licensed (where required by the local authority), and experienced.

---

## SECTION 7: HEALTH AND SAFETY

### 7.1 General
The Contractor shall comply with all statutory health and safety requirements and the Employer's project-specific safety regulations.

### 7.2 Electrical Safety
- Lock-out/tag-out procedures shall be enforced for all energized works.
- Permit-to-work systems shall be implemented for high-voltage and live electrical activities.
- Arc flash and shock hazard assessments shall be undertaken where applicable.

---

## SECTION 8: PROGRAM AND PROGRESS

### 8.1 Construction Program
The Tenderer shall submit a detailed construction program showing:
- Mobilization and site establishment
- Procurement and submittal lead times
- Installation sequences
- Testing and commissioning phases
- Milestones and key dates

### 8.2 Coordination with Other Trades
The electrical works shall be coordinated with civil, mechanical, and other specialist works to avoid clashes and ensure timely completion.

---

## SECTION 9: PRICING AND PAYMENT

### 9.1 Bill of Quantities
Pricing shall be submitted against the Bill of Quantities or Schedule of Rates provided, itemized to include:
- Supply and delivery
- Off-loading and storage
- Installation and fixing
- Testing and commissioning
- Documentation and handover

### 9.2 Provisional Sums and Prime Cost Sums
The Tenderer shall price provisional and prime cost sums as indicated, with applicable attendance and profit percentages clearly stated.

---

## SECTION 10: WARRANTY AND DEFECTS LIABILITY

### 10.1 Defects Liability Period
The Contractor shall be responsible for the rectification of defects in the electrical works for the duration specified in the Conditions of Contract.

### 10.2 Warranty
All equipment and materials shall be covered by the manufacturer's warranty, in addition to the Contractor's installation warranty.
