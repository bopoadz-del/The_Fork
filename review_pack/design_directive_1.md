---

## 1. Purpose

This directive instructs the Consultant to revise the parking layout within the scope of the current design package. The revision is required to address operational efficiency, compliance with applicable standards, and coordination with adjacent infrastructure elements (road alignment, drainage, and utility corridors) as shown in the issued-for-construction drawings.

---

## 2. Background

The current parking layout was developed during the preliminary design phase and has been carried forward into the detailed design. Subsequent coordination reviews, site observations, and updates to the road alignment (Roads 7–12) and stormwater drainage network have identified conflicts and sub-optimal arrangements that require the Consultant to revisit the parking geometry, circulation, and access points.

---

## 3. Scope of Revision Required

The Consultant shall revise the parking layout to address, at minimum, the following:

### 3.1 Geometric & Layout
- Re-verify all parking bay dimensions against the applicable local authority standards and the Employer's Requirements.
- Confirm minimum aisle widths for two-way circulation and emergency vehicle access.
- Reassess the number of standard, accessible (disabled), and designated bays to meet current demand projections and statutory minimums.
- Optimise the ingress/egress points to eliminate conflict with the revised road alignment and pedestrian circulation routes.

### 3.2 Drainage Coordination
- Ensure the revised parking surface grading is compatible with the stormwater drainage layout (refer to the latest issued stormwater drawings, series SW-200/SW-400).
- Confirm that surface runoff from the parking area is captured by the designated drainage network without ponding or overflow onto adjacent roads.

### 3.3 Utility Coordination
- Verify that the revised layout does not conflict with existing or proposed utility runs (MV cables, potable water, irrigation, street lighting) as shown in the coordinated utility drawings.
- Where conflicts are identified, the Consultant shall propose either a layout adjustment or a utility diversion and submit it for the Employer's review.

### 3.4 Pavement Design
- Reassess the pavement structural section for the parking area based on the revised geometry and any changes in traffic loading assumptions.
- Confirm subgrade CBR assumptions and pavement layer thicknesses are consistent with the geotechnical report and applicable AASHTO/local standards.

### 3.5 Signage, Marking & Safety
- Update the pavement marking plan to reflect the revised bay layout.
- Confirm provision of directional signage, speed control measures, and pedestrian crossing points.
- Verify compliance with fire safety access requirements (fire truck turning radius, hydrant accessibility).

## 5. Authority & Basis

This directive is issued under the Consultant's obligation to provide Services with reasonable skill, care, and diligence as set out in the Client/Consultant Model Services Agreement (FIDIC White Book). The Consultant's Scope of Services includes design coordination and revision in response to Employer instructions arising from project development and coordination requirements.

Per the document hierarchy of precedence, the Employer's Requirements take priority over the Consultant's prior design outputs; this directive constitutes an instruction to align the parking layout with the current Employer's Requirements and coordinated design baseline.
